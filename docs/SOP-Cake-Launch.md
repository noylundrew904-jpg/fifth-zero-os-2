# SOP: Cake Launch Readiness (Kitchen + Marketing)

**Purpose:** Guarantee that every new cake is fully developed, tested, costed,
and photographed/filmed — and that content is written, approved, and
scheduled — *before* the committed launch date, with no last-minute delays.

**Owner of this SOP:** Ops/Founder
**Applies to:** Kitchen/Pastry team, Marketing/Content team, QC, Ops

---

## 1. Roles

| Role | Who | Responsible for |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen Lead / Head Baker | Kitchen | Recipe dev, batch production, QC |
| Pastry/R&D Chef | Kitchen | Recipe iteration, tasting samples |
| QC Owner | Kitchen | Shelf-life, consistency, allergen check |
| Content Lead | Marketing | Shoot planning, copy, captions |
| Photographer/Videographer | Marketing | Product shoot execution |
| Social/Content Scheduler | Marketing | Posting calendar, platform prep |
| Final Approver | Founder/Ops | Go/No-Go decision, sign-off gate |

---

## 2. Stage-Gate Timeline (counting back from Launch Day = T-0)

Each stage has one **owner** and one **approver**. A stage cannot start until
the previous stage is signed off. This is what prevents delays — content
never gets shot on a recipe that isn't locked, and nothing gets posted before
kitchen confirms it can actually produce at scale.

| Stage | Day | Task | Owner | Deliverable | Approver / Sign-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | T-21 | Concept brief: flavor, occasion, price point, target volume | Founder + Kitchen Lead | 1-page brief | Founder |
| 1 | T-18 | Recipe development, first internal tasting | Pastry Chef | Tasting notes, 2-3 variants | Kitchen Lead |
| 2 | T-14 | **Recipe Lock** — finalize recipe, costing, shelf-life test started | Kitchen Lead | Locked recipe card + cost sheet | Founder ✅ Gate 1 |
| 3 | T-10 | Produce a hero sample batch, made exactly to the locked recipe, for the shoot | Kitchen | 1 photo-ready cake + 1 cut/cross-section version | Kitchen Lead |
| 4 | T-9 to T-7 | Content shoot (photo + video), get all raw assets | Marketing | Raw photo/video library | Content Lead |
| 5 | T-6 | **Creative Approval** — edited photos, reels/videos, captions drafted | Marketing | Final asset pack + draft copy | Founder ✅ Gate 2 |
| 6 | T-5 | Production trial run at real launch volume (not just a sample batch) | Kitchen | Trial batch + timing/yield notes | Kitchen Lead |
| 7 | T-4 | **Quality Sign-off** — final tasting of the scaled batch, allergen/label check | QC Owner | Signed QC checklist (below) | Kitchen Lead + Founder ✅ Gate 3 |
| 8 | T-3 | Finalize captions, hashtags, posting schedule, platform-specific edits | Marketing | Scheduled posts (in draft/queued state) | Content Lead |
| 9 | T-2 | Packaging, labeling, pricing in POS/menu ready | Kitchen + Ops | Packaged sample matches shoot | Ops |
| 10 | T-1 | **Final Go/No-Go meeting** — both teams confirm readiness | Kitchen Lead + Content Lead | Go/No-Go checklist signed | Founder ✅ Gate 4 (Launch Approval) |
| 11 | T-0 | **Launch** — production run starts, content goes live in sync | Both teams | Cake in-store + posts live | — |

> Rule: if any gate is missed, the launch date moves — content is **never**
> posted ahead of kitchen readiness, and production is **never** scaled up on
> an unapproved recipe.

---

## 3. The 4 Approval Gates (summary)

1. **Gate 1 – Recipe Lock (T-14):** Recipe, cost, and shelf-life are frozen.
   No further changes without restarting from Stage 1.
2. **Gate 2 – Creative Approval (T-6):** Photos/video/captions match the
   locked recipe and packaging exactly — no shooting a version that later
   changes.
3. **Gate 3 – Quality Sign-off (T-4):** The scaled-up production batch
   (not just the small sample) passes taste, consistency, and allergen
   checks.
4. **Gate 4 – Go/No-Go (T-1):** Joint kitchen + marketing confirmation that
   everything from Gates 1–3 is still true at launch scale, packaging is
   ready, and posts are queued.

Each gate needs an explicit written "Approved" (Slack/email/tracker), not a
verbal okay — this is what makes the trail auditable when a launch slips and
you need to know which stage caused it.

---

## 4. QC Checklist (Gate 3 — must all be checked before sign-off)

- [ ] Batch made at real launch volume/equipment (not a lab-scale sample)
- [ ] Taste/texture matches the Gate 1 locked recipe
- [ ] Shelf-life test completed for stated shelf life
- [ ] Allergen list confirmed and matches packaging/menu text
- [ ] Nutrition/ingredient label finalized (if required)
- [ ] Cost per unit matches approved cost sheet (no ingredient substitutions)
- [ ] Packaging fits and protects the actual cake size/shape

---

## 5. Delay-Prevention Rules

- **No shoot before recipe lock.** Photographing an unlocked recipe is the
  #1 cause of rework — if the recipe changes after the shoot, the shoot is
  redone.
- **Always shoot the real production version**, not a "prettier" one-off —
  what's photographed must be what customers actually get.
- **Build in a 2-day buffer** between Gate 3 (Quality Sign-off) and Gate 4
  (Go/No-Go) for QC to run a genuine scaled batch, not the same sample used
  for photos.
- **Marketing prepares scheduling in parallel with, not after, Kitchen's
  trial run** (Stages 6 and 8 run concurrently) — this is what keeps the
  timeline compressible if the launch date is tight.
- **One shared tracker** (sheet/board) listing all 11 stages with
  owner/status/due date, visible to both teams — no stage status lives only
  in someone's head or a private chat.
- **Daily standup from T-6 onward** (5 minutes, async is fine): each team
  reports "on track / at risk / blocked," so a slip is caught at T-6, not
  discovered at T-0.

---

## 6. Escalation

If any stage owner believes their deadline will be missed:
1. Flag it in the shared tracker **the same day** it becomes clear (not at
   the deadline itself).
2. Notify the Final Approver directly.
3. Founder/Ops decides: push the launch date, run a partial launch
   (e.g., in-store only, content delayed), or approve an accelerated path
   for the blocked stage.

A missed gate is never silently absorbed by the next stage's owner working
overtime — it's a scheduling decision made explicitly by the Final Approver.

---

## 7. Post-Launch (T+2 to T+7)

- Kitchen logs actual yield, waste, and any production issues from the real
  launch-day run.
- Marketing logs engagement on the launch content (views, saves, orders
  attributed to the campaign).
- Short retro: what caused any delay in this cycle, and which stage needs a
  longer buffer next time.
